How it works

You stay in charge of the books. The connector does the typing.

It learns your categories

Every business files expenses its own way. Correct a category once and the connector can remember it, by vendor or by item, so the next invoice comes in right.

Built not to make a mess

Your accounts matter more than speed, so the checks are built in.

  • Nothing posts without approvalEvery bill waits for you, and a dry run shows what will happen first.
  • No duplicate billsVendor and invoice number are checked against QuickBooks before posting.
  • Totals must add upA bill whose lines don't match the invoice total is held back for you to fix.
  • Your vendors, not new copiesExisting vendors are matched by name; new ones are flagged before they're created.
  • The original is keptThe invoice PDF is attached to the bill for your records and your accountant.
  • Access stays with youSign-in keys are stored on your own computer, and you can revoke access any time.

Questions

Which accounts does it work with?

Any Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox, and QuickBooks Online. QuickBooks Desktop isn't supported.

Can it read scanned or photographed invoices?

Yes. Typed PDFs are read directly, and scanned PDFs are read as images.

What happens to my email?

The connector only reads emails under the label you choose. The one change it makes is adding a label to invoices once they're in QuickBooks. It never sends, forwards or deletes email.

How do I stop it?

Remove its access in your Google account permissions and in your Intuit account's connected apps. It stops working immediately.
