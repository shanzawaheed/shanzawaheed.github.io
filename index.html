QuickBook Invoice Connector

Turn invoice emails into QuickBooks bills, with every line categorized and reviewed by you before anything is posted.

1. ReadFinds invoice emails under a Gmail label you choose and reads the attached invoice.
2. CategorizeProposes a bill with an expense category for each line, using rules you set.
3. ReviewYou check and correct categories. Your corrections become rules for next time.
4. PostApproved bills are created in QuickBooks with the invoice PDF attached.

Built for accuracy

Nothing is posted without approval. Duplicate invoices are detected, bills whose lines don't match the invoice total are blocked, and existing vendors are reused.
