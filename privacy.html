Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version
  • We only read the invoice emails you point us to.
  • We use them only to create the bills you approve.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising or AI training.

Who we are

QuickBook Invoice Connector (“the connector”, “we”) moves invoice emails from Gmail into QuickBooks Online as bills. Contact: shanzabutt90@gmail.com.

Data we access

Gmail. With your permission, the connector reads email messages that match a label or search you set (for example, an “Invoices” label), including their sender, subject, date, body text and attachments. It adds a label to messages once they're entered into QuickBooks. It does not send, delete or forward email.

QuickBooks Online. With your permission, the connector reads your chart of accounts, vendors and bills, and creates vendors, bills and bill attachments.

How we use it

Only to read invoice details, propose bills and categories for your review, and create the bills you approve. We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, we don't use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and we don't sell it.

Where it's stored

The connector runs on the computer of the person who operates it. Access tokens, downloaded invoice attachments, the review queue and category rules are stored locally on that computer. Bill data is stored in your QuickBooks company once posted. The operator may use an AI assistant to read invoices and suggest categories; invoice content is shared with that assistant only for this purpose.

Sharing

We don't share your data with third parties except as needed to provide the service described here, to comply with the law, or with your explicit consent.

Google API Services

The connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local copies of attachments and review data are kept only as long as needed to process invoices and can be deleted at any time. To have your data deleted, email us.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time: for Gmail at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for QuickBooks in your Intuit account's connected apps.

Security

Access tokens stay on the operator's computer and are never published. Connections to Google and Intuit use encrypted HTTPS.

Changes

If we update this policy, the effective date above will change.
